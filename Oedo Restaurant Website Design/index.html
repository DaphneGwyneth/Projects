Our Story

For over 12 years, Oedo Japanese Restaurant has been a hidden gem in Banawe, Quezon City — serving authentic Japanese cuisine crafted with care, tradition, and heart.

What started as a small neighborhood restaurant has grown into one of QC’s most loved Japanese dining destinations, known for generous portions, honest flavors, and warm service.

We believe great food doesn’t need shortcuts — only respect for ingredients and the people we serve.
